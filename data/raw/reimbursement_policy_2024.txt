NOVA DEMO COMPANY - EMPLOYEE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Version 2024, effective from 1 January 2024 through 31 December 2025.
Synthetic training document. All amounts and rules are fictional.

Eligible full-time employees may claim approved business expenses up to INR 30,000 per calendar year. The monthly home internet reimbursement limit is INR 1,000. Internet reimbursements count toward the annual limit. Personal purchases are excluded.

Claims must be submitted within thirty calendar days of the expense date. Original paper invoices must be submitted to Finance. Each claim requires approval from the employee's reporting manager before payment. Finance checks the invoice, eligibility, and available reimbursement balance. Employees should keep a copy of each claim and use its reference number when asking for an update.
