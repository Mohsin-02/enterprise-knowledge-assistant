NOVA DEMO COMPANY - EMPLOYEE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Version 2026, effective from 1 January 2026.
Synthetic training document. All amounts and rules are fictional.

Eligible full-time employees may claim approved business expenses up to INR 45,000 per calendar year. The monthly home internet reimbursement limit is INR 1,500. Internet reimbursements count toward the annual limit. Personal purchases are excluded.

Claims must be submitted within fifteen calendar days of the expense date. Digital invoices uploaded to the employee portal are accepted; paper originals are no longer required. Each claim requires approval from the employee's reporting manager before payment. An individual expense exceeding INR 10,000 also requires written Finance approval before the expense is incurred. Finance checks the invoice, eligibility, and available reimbursement balance. Employees should keep a copy of each claim and use its reference number when asking for an update.
